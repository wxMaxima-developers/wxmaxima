Detailed Description

Selecting some of the characters of the text in a cell's output (GH #825).

A drag in a cell's output used to select whole cells only, so copying half of an error message meant copying all of it. A drag that starts in text Maxima printed (TextCell::AllowsCharacterSelection()) now selects characters, as long as everything between where it started and where the pointer is now is such text.

How this stays out of the way of every other kind of selection:

  • The selection is worked out from scratch, from where the drag started and where the pointer is, on every mouse move (Worksheet::SelectOutputRect()). Nothing is carried over from the previous move, so dragging from characters out over maths (whole cells) and back gives characters again, without any state to get out of step.
  • The characters are a refinement of an ordinary selection of the cells they are in (DocumentCellPointers::SetSelectedText()), exactly like a selected block of matrix entries. Code that knows nothing about them sees those cells selected, and any other change to the selection – a click, a key, a drag over group cells – forgets them by itself.

outputthe first cell of a cell's output (GroupCell::GetLabel()), laid out.

Empty unless down is on a cell whose characters can be selected and every cell from there to the one up is at (or, if up is between or beside lines, the nearest one) can, too – the caller then selects whole cells, as before. Also empty if the drag covers no character at all, so that a drag that hasn't got anywhere yet selects what a click selects.
