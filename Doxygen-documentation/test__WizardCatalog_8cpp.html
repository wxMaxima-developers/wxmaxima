Detailed Description

Checks every entry of the catalog of standard wizards.

A wizard's rule refers to its fields by number (#1#, #2#, ...), so a field added or dropped without updating the rule silently leaves a "#3#" in the command, or a field the command ignores. Nothing else notices: the wizard opens and looks fine. No GUI and no Maxima needed.
