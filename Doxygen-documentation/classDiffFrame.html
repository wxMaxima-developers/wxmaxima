bool DiffFrame::FindNext ( bool  down)

Moves to the next (down) or previous match of the search field's text.

What the "Find next"/"Find previous" buttons, Enter/Shift+Enter in the search field and F3/Shift+F3 do. Continues in the pane the last match was found in and scrolls the other panes along. Returns false if nothing matched.
