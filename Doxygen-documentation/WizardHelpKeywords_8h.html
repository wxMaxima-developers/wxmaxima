std::vector< wxString > wxm::WizardHelpKeywords ( const wxString &  commandRule)

The words and operators of a wizard's command rule that might have help.

commandRule is the template the wizard fills in, e.g. "ode2(#1#,#2#,#3#);". The result lists, in the order they first appear and each once, every identifier ("ode2") and every operator the manual might document ("'", "''", "!", "#", "\\", and ";" or "$" when they separate two statements). Whether the manual actually has an entry for each of them is for the caller to check: this only does the text analysis, so it can be tested without a manual. Two things this deliberately leaves out: - The "#1#", "#2#", ... placeholders: they become the user's input. - The ";" or "$" that ends the command. Every wizard's command ends with one, so offering help on it says nothing about this particular wizard &ndash; and the Solve ODE wizard used to offer nothing else, since its "ode2" contains a digit and wasn't recognized as a word at all.
