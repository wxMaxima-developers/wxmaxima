
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
wxPdfDocument
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

wxMaxima's PDF export, and the PDF it puts on the clipboard, are made with
wxPdfDocument by Ulrich Telle, which is compiled into the wxMaxima binary.
wxMaxima uses a fork of it that adds tagged PDFs, font fallback and font
embedding, maintained by the wxMaxima team with the aim of contributing
these changes back.

The library was obtained from the following websites:
https://github.com/utelle/wxpdfdoc (the original)
https://github.com/wxMaxima-developers/wxpdfdoc (wxMaxima's fork)
